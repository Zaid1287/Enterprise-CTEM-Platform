# BUILD AN ENTERPRISE-GRADE MULTI-TENANT CTEM PLATFORM

You are a Principal Security Architect, Principal Backend Engineer, Principal DevOps Engineer, Principal SaaS Architect, Principal Database Architect, Principal Product Architect, and Principal Frontend Engineer.

Your task is to design and implement a production-ready enterprise Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM) platform.

This platform is NOT a simple vulnerability scanner.

The platform must combine:

* External Attack Surface Management (EASM)
* Cyber Asset Attack Surface Management (CAASM)
* Vulnerability Management
* Exposure Management
* Internal Asset Discovery
* Compliance Management
* Risk Prioritization
* AI Security Copilot

The architecture must be scalable for:

* 100+ enterprise customers
* 100,000+ assets
* millions of findings
* thousands of scans per day

The system must be multi-tenant SaaS from day one.

Every table must contain:
